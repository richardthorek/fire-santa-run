Design System · v2.0

Fire Santa Run

An Aussie summer-Christmas system for tracking Santa across the neighbourhood — recognisably part of the Station Manager family, just warmer, brighter and a lot more fun.

red · white · summer gold  /  Baloo 2 · Public Sans · JetBrains Mono  /  Lucide icons

01 — Design intent

Festive, but you'd trust it with your money.

Most of the app is public — parents on phones on a warm December night. It should feel joyful and alive. But brigades pay for it, so every surface also has to read as confident, modern and dependable.

Warm, not clip-art

Summer-Christmas warmth from red, white and beach-sun gold — festive cues stay tasteful and sparing.

One family

Shares Station Manager's neutrals, spacing, flat components and Lucide icons — so both tools feel built by the same team.

Phone-first & night-ready

The public tracker is designed at 375px, in the dark, with the map as the star and a festive night theme as the hero.

07 — Buttons

Flat, round-cornered, confident.

Gradients retired. Solid fills, 12px radius, Baloo 2 labels, a soft shadow that deepens on hover. Minimum 44px touch target. Never red-on-red.

08 — Components

Cards, badges & status.

Cards get one flat accent bar per module. Route status uses the real lifecycle from the codebase — draft → published → active → completed → archived — each mapped to a semantic colour.

10 — Terminology & voice

Festive and fun — but never flaky.

We're asking brigades for money, so the voice is warm and celebratory without sacrificing confidence. Consistent nouns across every surface.

Santa Run
the event — the night Santa visits. Not "the trip" or "the drive".
Route
the planned path Santa follows. One Santa Run runs a Route.
Stop
a point on the route. Public-facing word for "waypoint".
Brigade
the organisation. Say "brigade or community group" on first public mention.
Live Tracking
the public map experience. Not "broadcast" or "GPS feed" in UI.
Member
a person in a brigade. "Admin" only for elevated access.
Say this
  • "Track Santa live tonight."
  • "Plan your route in minutes."
  • "Trusted by volunteer brigades Australia-wide."
  • "No login needed — just tap and follow."
Not this
  • "Broadcast your GPS via the Mapbox layer."
  • "Configure waypoints on the Azure route entity."
  • "lol Santa go brrr 🎅🎅🎅" (over-the-top)
  • Implementation names in user-facing copy.
